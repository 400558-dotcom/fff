How to play

  1. Each GM starts with $22 in game credits.
  2. Bid any amount you want; the other GM can raise or pass.
  3. If both pass before any bid, the lot is skipped.
  4. Each roster has one QB, RB, WR, TE, and NFL team defense.
  5. Once both rosters are full, simulate the season.

Player overalls and the sample player pool are illustrative game ratings, not verified live 2026 season statistics. Add or update players as desired.
